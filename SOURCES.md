- **Title:** *Guide to creating a front of pack (FoP) nutrition label for pre-packed products sold through retail outlets*
- **Body:** Department of Health and Social Care, with the Food Standards Agency, Food Standards Scotland and the Welsh Government
- **URL:** https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5a80cd03ed915d74e33fc7c5/FoP_Nutrition_labelling_UK_guidance.pdf
- **Accessed:** 20 Sep 2026 (PDF downloaded and text extracted locally)
- **Defines:** The red/amber/green (HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW) criteria for fat, saturates, total sugars and salt, per 100 g for food and per 100 ml for drinks, plus per-portion criteria.
- **Values extracted verbatim from Annex 3, Tables 2 and 3:**

  *Table 2 — Criteria for 100 g of food*

### S2 — WHO guideline on free sugars · **Tier 1**
- **Title:** *WHO calls on countries to reduce sugars intake among adults and children* (news release accompanying *Guideline: sugars intake for adults and children*, WHO/NMH/NHD/15.2)
- **Body:** World Health Organization · **Date:** 4 March 2015
- **URL:** https://www.who.int/news/item/04-03-2015-who-calls-on-countries-to-reduce-sugars-intake-among-adults-and-children
- **Accessed:** 20 Sep 2026
- **Defines:**
  - Strong recommendation: *"adults and children reduce their daily intake of free sugars to less than 10% of their total energy intake"*
  - Conditional recommendation: *"A further reduction to below 5% or roughly 25 grams (6 teaspoons) per day would provide additional health benefits."*
  - **Free sugars definition (verbatim):** *"monosaccharides (such as glucose, fructose) and disaccharides (such as sucrose or table sugar) added to foods and drinks by the manufacturer, cook or consumer, and sugars naturally present in honey, syrups, fruit juices and fruit juice concentrates."*

### S7 — Nutri-Score · **Tier 1** (components only)
- **Title:** *Nutri-Score* · **Body:** Santé publique France · **Page updated:** 6 November 2025
- **URL:** http://www.santepubliquefrance.fr/nutri-score
- **Accessed:** 20 Sep 2026
- **Defines:** Per 100 g/ml scoring. Negative components: energy, saturated fatty acids, sugars, salt, **non-nutritive sweeteners**. Positive components: fibre, protein, fruits/vegetables/legumes. Five-colour A–E scale. 2023 European Scientific Committee algorithm update implemented 1 Jan 2024 (DE, BE, CH, NL), 5 Mar 2024 (LU), 16 Mar 2025 (FR).
- **Limitation:** the **point tables and category cut-offs were not retrieved** — the overview page does not carry them and the technical report was not opened. ### S8 — NOVA classification · **Tier 3**
- **Citation:** Monteiro CA, Cannon G, Levy RB, et al. *Ultra-processed foods: what they are and how to identify them.* Public Health Nutrition 2019;22(5):936–941. DOI 10.1017/S1368980018003762
- **URL:** https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/public-health-nutrition/article/ultraprocessed-foods-what-they-are-and-how-to-identify-them/E6D744D714B1FF09D5BCA3E74D53A185
- **Accessed:** 20 Sep 2026
- **Defines:** Groups 1–4. Group 4 (ultra-processed) = *"formulations of ingredients, mostly of exclusive industrial use"*. Practical identification rule: the ingredient list contains at least one food substance never used in kitchens (e.g. high-fructose corn syrup, hydrogenated oils, hydrolysed proteins) **or** a cosmetic additive (flavours, colours, emulsifiers, sweeteners, thickeners).
- **Tier note:** this is the defining paper, but it is a single peer-reviewed article, not a regulator instrument. Under this session's rules it may add a note, **not set a threshold**, unless a tier-1/2 source agrees. No retrieved tier-1 source uses NOVA.

### S9 — Gulf nutritional labelling standard (draft) · **Tier 1, draft status**
- **Title:** *GSO 05/FDS 2233 — Requirements of Nutritional Labelling* (Final Draft Standard, prepared by GSO Technical Committee TC05; supersedes Gulf Technical Regulation 2233/2012 when approved)
- **Body:** GCC Standardization Organization
- **URL:** https://images.chemycal.com/Media/Files/ARE451_EN_1_1.pdf
- **Accessed:** 20 Sep 2026 (PDF downloaded, text extracted locally)
- **Defines, verbatim:**
  - §3.5.1 **Added sugars** — *"sugars that are either added during the processing of foods, or are packaged as such, and include sugars (free, mono- and disaccharides), sugars from syrups and honey, and sugars from concentrated fruit or vegetable juices that are in excess of what would be expected from the same volume of 100 percent fruit or vegetable juice of the same type."*
  - **NRVs (Table 3):** Energy 2000 kcal · Protein 50 g · Total fat 70 g · **Saturates 20 g**
  - **Declared-value tolerance limits (Table 6)** — *for saturated and monounsaturated fatty acids:* ≤4 g/100 g → ±20%; >4 g → ±8 g. *For sodium:* ≤0.5 g → ±20%; >0.5 g → ±0.15 g. *For salt:* ≤1.25 g → ±20%; >1.25 g → ±0.375 g. *For sugars and fibre:* ≤10 g → ±2 g; 10–40 g → ±20%; >40 g → ±8 g.
  - Scope exclusions include fresh produce, fresh meat/poultry/fish, single-nutrient foods, bottled water, and foods for special dietary uses.
- **Status caveat:** this is a **final draft circulated for comment**, explicitly *"subject to alteration and modification and may not be referred to as a Gulf Technical Regulation until approved"*. The published GSO 2233:2021 is behind the GSO store paywall (§3).

### S13 — EU authorised health claims · **Tier 1**

- **URL:** https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX%3A32012R0432

### S15 — EFSA on food additives · **Tier 1**
- **Title:** *Food additives* topic page · **Body:** European Food Safety Authority
- **URL:** https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/topics/topic/food-additives
- **Accessed:** 20 Sep 2026
- **Defines:** EFSA provides scientific advice supporting Commission authorisation; risk assessment covers chemical and biological properties, potential toxicity and dietary exposure. ### S17 — EU food information to consumers · **Tier 1**
- **Title:** Regulation (EU) No 1169/2011 on the provision of food information to consumers — **Annex III, *Foods whose labelling must include one or more additional particulars***

- **Defines, verbatim — the mandatory particulars that constitute an EU warning label:**

## 4. Honest assessment of this evidence base

1. **The brief was not completed.** Roughly 20 of the ~25 source families requested were opened. The gaps are concentrated in exactly the places Noura is weakest: **supplements, cosmetics, additive-level evaluations, and Gulf-specific regulator documents other than excise and the draft labelling standard.**
2. **No tier-2 source was retrieved.** Every threshold below therefore rests on tier-1 regulator instruments or is explicitly marked unsourced. No systematic review or meta-analysis was opened, so no rule here is justified by pooled clinical evidence.
3. **The most UAE-specific nutrition instrument retrieved (S5) is a marketing-restriction model, not a dietary standard.** It is designed to decide what may be advertised to children, which is a stricter and differently-motivated question than "is this a good choice for an adult shopper". RUBRIC.md uses it for category *structure* and as a conservative cross-check, not as the primary threshold source.
4. **The only binding UAE numeric thresholds retrieved are fiscal** (S10), not nutritional. They are used, but their provenance is stated plainly wherever they appear.
5. **The per-portion half of the UK criteria (S1) is unused** because Noura holds no serving-size data — a limitation recorded in RUBRIC.md §9.
6. **The additive flag list is a floor, not a screen.** It contains every additive a retrieved instrument puts a warning label on or a retrieved EFSA opinion raised a concern about, and nothing else. An additive absent from it has not been cleared by Noura; it has not been looked at. RUBRIC §5.1 says so to the user.
7. **The EMRO table was transcribed wrongly the first time.** One of three uncertain rows turned out to contain a number that was not a threshold at all (S5, category 10). The lesson recorded: a table read from flattened PDF text is not evidence, and the `evidence/` directory now holds the extraction output so the next reader can check rather than trust.
